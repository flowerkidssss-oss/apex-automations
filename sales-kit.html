02 — The Apex System

Four automations. One system.

We build and manage a four-part automation stack that runs in the background while you're under the hood. No apps to learn. No tech headaches.

01

Missed Call Text-Back

Every missed call gets an instant text message — within seconds. Lets the customer know you'll be with them shortly and keeps them from calling the next shop.

02

After-Hours Auto-Response

Calls and messages outside business hours get a smart automated reply with your hours and a prompt to book or call back — so you never lose a late-night inquiry.

03

Appointment Follow-Up Sequence

After a visit, a short message sequence checks in on the customer, reminds them of upcoming service needs, and keeps your shop top of mind when they need you next.

04

Review Request Campaign

Satisfied customers get a friendly, well-timed ask for a Google review. More 5-star reviews mean more organic traffic and more trust from new customers searching online.

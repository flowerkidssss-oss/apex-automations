# Apex Automations — Ad Script V1
**Format:** 30-second vertical (9:16) + square (1:1) crop
**Tone:** Conversational female VO, slight authority, mix of pain + aspiration
**Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed

---

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT

**[0–3s — HOOK]**
> "Your shop is losing thousands of dollars every single month."

**[3–8s — AGITATE]**
> "Every missed call, every lead that texts and never hears back — that's a job someone else is getting."

**[8–16s — SOLUTION]**
> "Apex Automations installs a done-for-you system that responds to every lead in seconds, follows up for seven days, and books the job — automatically. No extra staff. No extra work."

**[16–22s — ASPIRATION]**
> "Imagine waking up to new appointments already on the calendar. That's what our clients get."

**[22–27s — CREDIBILITY + URGENCY]**
> "We set everything up. You just run your shop."

**[27–30s — CTA]**
> "See how it works. Link in bio."

---

## VISUAL STYLE DIRECTION
- **Color palette:** Near-black backgrounds, gold text accents, white body text
- **Mood:** Premium but grounded. Not Silicon Valley. A shop owner should feel like this was made FOR them.
- **Footage style:** Mix of realistic AI-generated shop/mechanic scenes + clean UI mockup screens
- **Motion:** Subtle — slow push-ins, smooth cuts, no jarring transitions
- **Captions:** Bold, centered, large — auto-styled like high-performing TikTok ads

---

## MUSIC DIRECTION
- Understated, slightly tense build in first 8s (pressure feeling)
- Smooth, confident groove from 8s onward (things clicking into place)
- No lyrics — VO takes priority
- Reference vibe: lo-fi cinematic / dark trap instrumental

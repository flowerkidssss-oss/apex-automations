## Package: "The Apex System"

### What's Included

#### Flow 1 — Missed Call Text-Back
**Trigger:** Incoming call that goes unanswered
**Action:** Instant automated SMS within 60 seconds
**Message:** "Hey, this is [Shop Name]! Sorry we missed your call. Reply here or book online: [link]"
**Why it works:** 78% of customers buy from whoever responds first. Most shops respond in hours or never.

---

#### Flow 2 — New Lead Follow-Up Sequence
**Trigger:** Form fill, quote request, or new contact added to CRM
**Sequence:**
- Day 0 (immediate): "Thanks for reaching out! We'll get you a quote within a few hours."
- Day 1: "Hey [Name], just following up on your quote request. Still interested?"
- Day 3: "Still thinking it over? We're offering [incentive] this week — grab it here."
- Day 7: "Last check-in from [Shop Name]. If the timing's off, no worries — we'll be here."
**Why it works:** Most sales happen on follow-up 4–7. Almost nobody follows up that many times manually.

---

#### Flow 3 — Review Request Automation
**Trigger:** Job marked complete (manual tag or POS integration)
**Timing:** SMS sent 24 hours after completion
**Message:** "Thanks for coming in, [Name]! If you loved the work, a quick Google review would mean the world to us: [link]. Takes 30 seconds 🙏"
**Why it works:** 90% of customers never leave reviews because they're never asked. This removes that gap.

---

#### Flow 4 — Customer Reactivation Campaign
**Trigger:** Customer hasn't visited or been contacted in 90 days
**Sequence:**
- Message 1: "Hey [Name]! It's been a while — your car probably needs some love. We're running [offer] this month. Want to book?"
- Message 2 (7 days later if no reply): "Last call for [offer] at [Shop Name] — spots filling up this week."
**Why it works:** Existing customers are 5x easier to close than new ones. Most shops ignore their list.

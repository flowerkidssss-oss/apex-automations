# Apex Automations — Brand & Positioning

## Name
**Apex Automations**

Why it works:
- "Apex" = the fastest line through a corner in racing — precision, speed, peak performance
- Subtle nod to the auto/car world without being locked in
- Sounds premium and scalable if you expand beyond auto shops
- Short, memorable, easy to say

## Tagline
**"We automate the follow-up. You keep the customers."**

Alt options:
- "More leads answered. More jobs booked. Less work for you."
- "Your shop, on autopilot."
- "We plug the leaks. You keep the revenue."

## Target Client
**Auto detailers and body shops across the United States**

Profile:
- 1–10 employees
- Owner-operated or small team
- $200K–$2M annual revenue
- Already busy — doesn't have time to manage leads manually
- Probably using: phone, email, maybe a basic booking app
- NOT using: automated follow-up, CRM sequences, review automation
- Pain: missing calls, slow responses, low Google reviews, dormant customer list

## Positioning Statement
Apex Automations installs done-for-you lead response and follow-up systems for auto detail and body shops. We close the gap between a new lead and a booked job — automatically, without hiring more staff.

## Voice & Tone
- Direct. No fluff.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Speaks the shop owner's language ("missed calls," "booked jobs," "Google reviews")
- Never corporate. Never overcomplicated.
- Think: smart friend who knows tech, talking to a shop owner

## Core Message Pillars
1. **Speed** — Respond to leads in seconds, not hours
2. **Simplicity** — They don't touch the system, we run it
3. **Revenue** — Every message about ROI, not features

## Brand Colors
- Background: #0a0a0a (near black)
- Cards/surfaces: #111111
- Accent/highlight: #e8c547 (gold)
- Text: #ffffff / #cccccc
